How it works & privacy

When enabled, a recognized FINISH sends your accumulated draft to the server LLM and speaks its suggestion. FINISH is not added to the draft. Add each word first, or enable automatic adding above. FINISH uses the default motion model: choose FINISH → Prepare practice after using the five-word server preview. Recognition and interpretation can be wrong. Empty, changed, or unclear drafts are not spoken.

Only draft text is sent to the local model; camera frames are not sent and drafts are not saved automatically.

The LLM may change the meaning or guess incorrectly. It cannot verify your signs or translate ASL grammar. Suggestions are never applied automatically. Speech is automatic only when you enable the FINISH setting.

Built for inclusion

Communication should feel effortless.

HandSpeak Web is a capstone prototype that uses computer vision to help bridge everyday communication between deaf, hard-of-hearing, and hearing individuals. It recognizes selected static letters and offers an experimental pretrained vocabulary for moving signs. Face landmarks are displayed for visual feedback; they are not used to interpret identity, emotions, or ASL grammar.

Help, privacy, and recognition limits

Letters: hold a supported letter steady. Words: select Words in motion, sign once, and pause. Watch the examples first; use Record a word if automatic capture misses the movement.

Camera frames and personal gesture examples stay on your device. The optional five-word preview sends recorded hand-landmark coordinates to the server for in-memory recognition; it does not upload video or save those coordinates. Saving sends your message and letter-detection statistics to this server. Interpret draft sends only the reviewed text to a local model on this server; it does not upload camera footage or save the draft automatically. Guest history is linked to a browser cookie. Clearing cookies removes access to guest history. Existing account sessions retain access to their saved data. You can export or delete your own saved messages.

Administrators can read submitted feedback. Public recognition, learning, and saving do not require sign-in.

This is an experimental ASL learning and recognition application, not a complete language translator. Similar and unsupported signs can be misidentified. Extra-word recognition is a preview. Always review suggestions before adding or speaking them.
